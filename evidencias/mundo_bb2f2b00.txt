A wide‑angle view from below the seabed shows the sprawling metropolis of crystal domes reflected in shimmering turquoise and coral hues, while the shadowy black of the deep looms beneath. In the foreground, the massive silhouette of a leviathan coils, its silver scales glinting like moonlit plates against the dark water. Orange bioluminescent filaments ripple across the creature’s dorsal fin, cutting a stark line against the muted background. Soft, diffused light filters through the water, creating a tranquil, almost ethereal glow that bathes the city and the beast in a dreamlike, painterly style reminiscent of late‑20th‑century surrealism.
